# Tuition Billing & Collections Policy (SIS-FIN-003)

## Billing
Tuition is billed per term. The student record's tuition_status shows the state of the account:
- paid: the balance is zero.
- partial: at least 25% has been paid and the rest is on an approved payment plan.
- unpaid: no payment has been received for the term.

## Reminders
1. 7 days after the due date, an email reminder goes to the student's parent or guardian
   (the contact linked to the student in the CRM).
2. 21 days after the due date, the parent or guardian receives a phone call, and the call
   is logged as a CRM activity.
3. 45 days after the due date, the account is referred to the Bursar. The student may not
   enrol for the next term until the account is settled or a payment plan is agreed.

## Communication rules
Always contact the parent or guardian through their preferred channel recorded in the CRM.
Never discuss balances with anyone not listed as the student's parent or guardian.
