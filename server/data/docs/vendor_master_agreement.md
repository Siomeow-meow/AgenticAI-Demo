# Vendor Master Agreement: Standard Terms (PROC-VMA-2026)

This agreement applies to all vendors in the ERP vendor master unless a signed addendum says otherwise.

## Payment terms
Vendors are paid according to the terms in their vendor record (Net 15, 30, 45 or 60 days
from the invoice date). Invoices must quote a valid purchase order number, or they may be
returned unpaid.

## Pricing
Prices are fixed for 12 months from contract signature. Price increases need 60 days'
written notice and may not exceed 5% per year.

## Delivery and quality
- Goods must be delivered within the lead time stated on the purchase order.
- Damaged or wrong items must be replaced within 7 days at the vendor's expense.
- A delivery is late if it arrives more than 3 business days after the expected date.

## Vendor rating
Vendors are rated quarterly from 1.0 to 5.0 on quality, delivery and price. A vendor rated
below 3.5 is placed on probation: new orders above $5,000 need Procurement Manager approval.
A vendor rated below 3.0 for two consecutive quarters may be terminated with 30 days' notice.

## Confidentiality and data
Vendors may only use our data to fulfil orders. They must delete it within 30 days of the
contract ending, following our Data Retention Policy.
