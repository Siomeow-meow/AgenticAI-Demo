# Expense & Receipt Processing Policy (FIN-EXP-004)

## 1. Scope
Covers purchases paid on the spot (company card or cash) where we receive a store receipt
rather than a vendor invoice.

## 2. How to record a receipt
1. Check the vendor exists in the ERP vendor master. If it does not, send the receipt to
   Procurement for vendor onboarding before recording it.
2. Record the receipt in the ERP as an **invoice** with status **paid**. Use the receipt
   number as the invoice number and the receipt total (including tax) as the amount.
3. Create a matching **purchase order** with status **received** (a "retroactive PO"), so
   that every spend has a PO for audit purposes. Use the receipt date as the order date.
4. Attach a short report of what was recorded for the Finance team's monthly review.

## 3. Approval limits
- Up to $500: no extra approval needed.
- $500 to $2,000: the department head approves within 5 business days.
- Over $2,000: card purchases are not allowed. Raise a normal purchase order instead.

## 4. Deadlines
Receipts must be recorded within 10 days of purchase. Uploaded receipt images are deleted
90 days after their data is recorded (see the Data Retention Policy).
